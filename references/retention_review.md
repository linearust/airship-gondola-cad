# Retention and assembly — AG

The separate output-bearing caps and horn rear straps are removed. This is a
nominal CAD design and assembly review, not qualification of the received
bearings, prepared metal parts, PA12 fit, friction, creep or strength.

## Output bearings

Each rigid bearing cup opens toward its rotor. An integral outer shoulder
retains the bearing outward. A bought flanged metal spacer between the rotor
and bearing inner ring limits inward withdrawal. The rotor's 1.5 mm thick integral end flanges
meet broad frame stops; shaft grip remains the separate split-clamp joint.
The bearing locations, gear centres and removable paired servo module remain
unchanged. There is no printed snap finger, shaft groove or assumed press fit.

- Four caps, four M2 screws and four M2 nuts are replaced by four spacers.
- The former tall cap-screw tabs are removed from the frame.
- The spacer's narrow end faces the bearing; its flange faces the rotor.
  It must contact the inner ring without touching the shield or outer ring.
- The cups retain full radial bearing support throughout the checked axial
  movement. The carrier/frame stops are low-load rubbing stops, not a
  preloaded bearing stack. Nominal clearance is not a delivered fit guarantee.

The selected spacer is **HIROSUGI F3035-5105T**, a turned free-cutting steel
bush with trivalent chromate finish: bore 3 mm (+0.1/0), neck OD 3.5 mm (+0/-0.1),
flange OD 5 mm, flange thickness 1 mm and neck length 0.5 mm; total length 1.5 mm.
The manufacturer's drawing measures neck length separately from flange
thickness. Published bore and neck tolerances do not establish the other
lengths, chamfers or actual contact land. Its use here is as an axial spacer,
not as a plain bearing. The manufacturer lists a 50-piece minimum; Korean
order acceptance and supply are unconfirmed.
[Manufacturer table](https://hirosugi.co.jp/shop/g/gF2030-5805T/) ·
[Drawing](https://hirosugi.co.jp/img/goods/1/Mbush_d.png).

The retained ISC MR63ZZ reference permits inner abutment OD up to 3.7 mm and
housing opening at least 5.4 mm. These limits guide the nominal 3.5 mm spacer neck
and 5.6 mm outer-shoulder opening. They do **not** certify the generic bearings
selected in the cart. Check the received race lands, journal fit, free rotation
and assembled endplay. Do not substitute an ordinary M3 washer.
[ISC reference](https://www.nskmicro.co.jp/products/bearing/bearing_size_pdf/single_row_mm.pdf).

### Staged assembly

Install bearings from inside before the motor carrier. Put the spacers on the
separate output shafts with their narrow ends toward the bearings. Hold the
shafts retracted clear of the carrier and stage the spacers slightly outward
within their available axial gap. Insert the carrier laterally between the
posts, advance the shafts into its clamps, then tighten and fit the output
gear. Removal reverses this order after disconnecting/freeing wiring.
Do not try to push the flanged spacers radially into the finished narrow gap.
Native tests check the staged paths; actual handling, shaft fit and preparation
still require a bench trial before assembling the full vehicle.

## Horn adapter

Each KST 0415.13 horn retains its bought spline and original OEM retaining
screw. Only the existing nominal 1 mm tip hole at radius 13.2 mm is enlarged to
1.8 mm. Support the blade, preserve the hole centre and deburr without
countersinking. Reject distortion, cracks or insufficient screw-head bearing.
This is explicitly workshop preparation, not a factory interface.
[KST arm](https://kstservos.com/products/0415-13-aluminium-servo-arm-for-4mm-15t-servo).

A shared M1.6x8 DIN84 screw passes from the metal rear face through the horn
and adapter; a shared M1.6 nut seats on the adapter front. The nominal stack is
5.2 mm grip + 1.3 mm nut + 1.5 mm tip. The adapter hole is 2 mm to avoid imposing a
second precise hole alignment. Hub and tip datums still locate the adapter;
the short metal input stub and gear bore remain unchanged. The adapter's outer
tip region is reduced while preserving at least the general 1.5 mm wall target.
No separate rear strap, washer or new fastener family is needed.

The remaining nominal web between the enlarged hole and the adjacent 0.8 mm
hole is 0.4 mm. This alone neither proves failure nor establishes strength.
Alloy grade, drilling quality, contact pressure, backlash, fatigue and loaded
retention are unqualified. Check both torque directions and recheck the joint
for loosening and PA12 creep. Clearance checks do not settle those questions.

Install the OEM spline screw first. At neutral, insert the tip screw from
behind the metal horn, approach the adapter axially, then fit the front nut.
For service remove the nut, withdraw the tip screw and release the adapter;
no access through the solid input shaft is assumed.

A rigid large washer was rejected as the default: it adds a hardware type,
retains the piece count and needs a reliable shared horn/printed support plane.
An integral snap jaw would need extra entry clearance and compliant closure.
Direct through-fastening reduces parts without that spring or contact-plane
sensitivity, at the explicit cost of preparing the metal horn.
