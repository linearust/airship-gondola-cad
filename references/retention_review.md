# Bearing retention review — AF

Reviewed 2026-09-23 for the request to simplify fasteners and prefer suitable
standard retainers. Retain the four removable bearing caps and their existing
M2 screw/nut pairs. The examined alternatives do not simplify the complete
assembly while preserving the selected plain Ø3 rods, generic 3 × 6 × 2.5 mm
bearings and accessible printed seats. This is not a claim that every available
standard bearing arrangement is unsuitable.

## Separate retention functions

| Interface | Current function |
| --- | --- |
| Frame shoulder and removable cap | Capture the bearing **outer ring in the frame**, in both axial directions. The nominal Ø5.6 opening is intended to clear the shields and inner ring; confirm actual race lands on the purchased generic bearings. |
| Motor-carrier split clamps | Grip the two separate Ø3 output stubs for torque transmission and axial retention relative to the rotating carrier. |
| Carrier/frame end faces | Limit carrier axial travel with nominal 0.5 mm clearance each way. These are rubbing stops, not bearing inner-ring clamps or a zero-endplay bearing stack. |

A collar or retaining ring on the shaft cannot by itself replace the outer-ring
cap. Conversely, the cap does not establish shaft grip. Geometry checks do not
qualify friction, loads, wear, PA12 creep or the purchased fits.

## Examined alternatives

- **Shaft retaining ring:** MISUMI's DIN471 size-3 example requires a Ø2.8 mm
  shaft groove and at least 0.5 mm groove width. It would introduce precision
  groove preparation on the selected plain rod and still leave outer-ring
  housing retention unresolved. No suitable grooving operation is assumed.
  [MISUMI DIN471 table](https://th.misumi-ec.com/en/vona2/detail/110310992519/?Tab=codeList).
- **Housing retaining ring:** the examined Ochiai RTW-6 requires a Ø6.25
  +0.04/0 mm groove, nominal 0.5 mm width and has a listed installed clearance
  diameter of only 2.5 mm. It does not provide clearance for the Ø3 shaft or
  the present Ø5.6 shield-clear opening. Its groove is also a substantially
  finer interface than the current finishable PA12 seat. This rejection applies
  to that ring, not all internal-ring designs.
  [Ochiai RTW dimensional drawing and table](https://mx.c.misumi-ec.com/en/book/OCH1_M01_KGT/pdf/0010.pdf).
- **Flanged bearing:** a flange provides one axial housing stop. Reverse
  retention would still need a retainer or a redesigned shaft/inner-ring stack;
  exchanging only the bearing does not close that load path.
- **Shaft collar:** a collar addresses shaft/inner-ring retention and still
  needs appropriate shield clearance and outer-ring capture. For scale, the
  examined Ruland MCL-3-A has a 3 mm bore but a 16 mm outside diameter and 9 mm
  width, exceeding the present 9.6 mm cup diameter. Smaller collars may exist;
  none has been selected as a complete replacement arrangement.
  [Ruland MCL-3-A](https://www.ruland.com/mcl-3-a.html).
- **Press fit alone:** do not replace positive retention with an assumed raw
  printed interference. Fits depend on measured dimensions and operating
  conditions; excessive interference can reduce bearing clearance, while a
  loose fit can permit creep. Actual shaft and housing fits remain prototype
  checks. [NSK fits and internal clearance](https://www.nsk.com/tools-resources/abc-bearings/fits-and-internal-clearance/).

A radial sliding cap would require shaft clearance during removal, guide rails,
an end stop and a lock, while preserving concentric outer-race contact. An
integral snap cap adds elastic retention and tolerance requirements at the
small bearing interface. Neither is established as simpler or more robust than
the current cap and one common fastener pair. Keep the removable cap until a
complete alternative demonstrates assembly access, shield clearance, positive
retention and acceptable fits together.

This record covers bearing retention only. Optical mounting and horn-coupling
changes under the AF review are governed by their implementation and validation;
this document does not assert their completion or qualification.
