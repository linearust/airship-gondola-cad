# Universal equipment carriers — BF

The current common-plate design is recorded in the [BF plate review](dense_mount_review.md).
The [BB review](square_mount_review.md) retains the earlier pattern and audit.
Battery, FC and navigation use three identical 64 mm square prints with an
integral rail shoe; the optional power platform uses the same plate template on
its own integral portal. Source contracts own exact dimensions and permitted uses.

The plate and all mounting openings have quarter-turn and mirror symmetry.
The rail shoe, rail-clamp direction and installed devices do not inherit that
symmetry automatically. Four fixed FC holes preserve its 25.5 mm pattern; 24
slots provide the adjustable common patterns, P-AS attachment and support feet.
No projecting utility tabs, optical-only bores or dedicated tie holes are added.

The compact optical pedestal and optional power portal use different feet and
registration bounds. Do not copy the old AY two-axis portal assumptions or treat
nominal slot alignment as physical pointing qualification. The selected Mini
uses an offset position on the navigation carrier underside, clear of both clamp
tool approaches and optional-power fastener service. Two separately positioned
adhesive strips retain 120 mm² of nominal contact while leaving the new middle
slot open. The battery's three continuous patches total 376 mm². These areas do
not qualify physical retention. Do not restore older offsets or tape rectangles
from historical revisions.

Preserve the complete slot lands, FC bearing annuli, P-AS slot seats, fastener-head
access and continuous adhesive allocations. Slots provide geometric adjustment;
actual clamp contact, electrical insulation, loaded retention and device stacking
still need physical verification. Tests must inspect the saved solids as well as
source metadata and must reject missing material or blocked openings.
